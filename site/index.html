How it moves

Two fixed side rails carry a middle beam that travels forward and back. A motorised hoist trolley runs left and right along that beam, and the chain hoist lifts the load. Together they cover the whole batching floor.

  • FIXEDLeft & right standardsISMB 200 rails, 4 m each
  • Y-AXISMiddle beam forward / back2000 mm travel
  • X-AXISHoist trolley left / right1200 mm travel
  • Z-AXISLiftGround to 2nd floor, 4000 mm

01Front view: existing BPD plant tower

No civil modification

The existing tower stands about 6 m tall with yellow UltraTech bags stacked at ground level. The fixed rails bolt to the existing building columns at 4.5 m height, so the plant keeps running during installation.

02Isometric design

Fixed rails, the moving middle beam and the hoist trolley shown over the batching plant, with the bag loading zone, cement silo and access stair to the second floor.

03Top view blueprint

Plan layout of the X-Y coverage area, 2000 mm × 1200 mm, over the aggregate bins, cement silo and mixer unit.
